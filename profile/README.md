## ✨ Highlights

- **Pixel, everywhere:** Pixel wallpapers, lockscreen clocks, boot animations and sound effects on non-Pixel devices
- **Evolver:** our in-house settings app, with hundreds of tweaks for colors, fonts, status bar, lockscreen, per-app volume, icon packs and more
- **Now Playing with lyrics:** song recognition and lyrics on the lockscreen, even on non-Pixel hardware
- **Play Integrity ready:** built-in spoofing support, configurable from Evolver
- **Always current:** monthly Android security patches and fast rebases
- **Fully open source:** every line is public. Audit it, fork it, learn from it

See the full list at [evolution-x.org/features](https://evolution-x.org/features).

## 📲 Install

1. Check that your device is supported at [evolution-x.org/devices](https://evolution-x.org/devices)
2. Download a build from [cdn.evolution-x.org](https://cdn.evolution-x.org)
3. Follow the install guide for your device at [wiki.evolution-x.org](https://wiki.evolution-x.org)

> [!WARNING]
> Back up your data before flashing. Installing a custom ROM can void your warranty.

## 🤝 Get involved

- **Maintain a device:** we especially need help bringing devices up on Android 17 (`cnb`). Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/evolution-x-670512508871639041).
- **Contribute to Evolver:** the most approachable place to start if you know Java/Kotlin and Android UI. Fork [packages_apps_Evolver](https://github.com/Evolution-X/packages_apps_Evolver), branch from `cnb`, and use Gerrit-style commit messages.
- **Translate:** no technical knowledge needed. Pick your language on [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/Evolution_X).
